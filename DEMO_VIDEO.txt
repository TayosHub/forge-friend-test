Demo video drop-in
==================
Licensed Forge ExerciseMedia clips are NOT used on the public page.

To show a real clip in the exercise-demo phone frame, place a short muted
H.264 MP4 named exactly:

  demo.mp4

next to index.html (same folder). The page autoplays/loops it when present;
otherwise it shows the placeholder: "Demo video plays here in the app".

Intended source: a self-shot clip from Tayo (redistributable).
